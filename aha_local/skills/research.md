# Research

- Write specific queries: the place, the year, the official name; the local language for local things.
- Find options on aggregators and review sites, then confirm on the primary source (the venue, the airline, the government page).
- Open at least two pages for anything that matters, and check each page's date.
- Prices change by the hour and depend on dates, route, people and class: quote exactly what the page showed, for those parameters, with the site and when you checked.
- Sources disagree: say so, and which you believe and why. Couldn't verify: say exactly what.
